# 2025-07-22 LTLyra

【段落 1】 **China – AI: Reviving a giant with H20 chip availability** 中国AI：借H20芯片供应复苏，唤醒沉睡的巨人 The lifting of US export restrictions on NVIDIA ’s less advanced AI GPUs (e.g., H20) to China removes a key near-term headwind outlined in our recent China AI Blue Paper. It should now be easier for consumer/ enterprise AI applications to develop faster in China, a significant step forward. 美国取消对 英伟达 较低端AI GPU（如H20）的对华出口限制，消除了我们近期《中国AI蓝皮书》中所指出的关键短期阻力。这将使中国消费级及企业级AI应用得以更快发展，成为重要进展。 --- 【段落 2】 China – AI: Significant step forward. We recently explored the rapid development of AI in China in our Blue Paper report, *China – AI: The Sleeping Giant Awakens*. We noted its diverging path from the US approach, its focus on bringing AI to market, impacting the AI ecosystem and influencing AI standards, rather than building the most powerful AI capabilities. However, we added that US restrictions on essential AI chips remained near-term headwinds that might lead to some China AI capex cuts. Now, news that the US will permit exports of lower-level AI chips (e.g., H20) to China prompts us to update our views. This development also feeds into our China emerging frontiers theme, a cornerstone of which is the rapid advancement in technology and the burgeoning digital economy, powered by AI chips. 中国AI：迈出重要一步。我们在近期蓝皮书《中国AI：沉睡的巨人觉醒》中探讨了中国AI的快速发展。我们指出，中国AI选择了一条与美国不同的道路：专注于将AI推向市场、影响AI生态与标准，而非单纯打造最强算力。然而，我们此前也提到，美国对关键AI芯片的限制仍是短期逆风，可能导致中国AI资本支出缩减。如今，美国将允许较低端AI芯片（如H20）对华出口的消息，促使我们更新观点。这一进展也契合我们“中国新前沿”主题，其核心正是由AI芯片驱动的技术快速进步与 数字经济 蓬勃发展。 --- 【段落 3】 Good news for China's AI developers. Now that shipments of H20 chips look likely to resume, we think that Rmb380bn capex in 2025 from major Chinese CSPs looks to be on track — we even see some upside if US GPU supply is sufficient. The capex amount is also close to NVIDIA ’s estimate of a US$50bn revenue opportunity for AI accelerators in 2025. This development is key to our thesis that China is bringing AI to the mass market via apps, LLMs and other models, and embedding AI in the day-to-day user experience. 对中国AI开发者而言是利好。随着H20芯片供应有望恢复，我们预计2025年中国头部云服务商3800亿元人民币的资本支出将按计划推进；若美国GPU供应充足，甚至存在上行空间。该支出规模与 英伟达 预估的2025年中国AI加速器500亿美元市场机会高度吻合。这一进展支撑了我们的核心观点：中国正通过应用、大语言模型等方式将AI推向大众市场，并融入日常用户体验。 --- 【段落 4】 Good news for the US semiconductor industry. The China AI capex amount is also close to NVIDIA ’s estimate of a US$50bn revenue opportunity for AI accelerators in 2025. The US AI companies NVIDIA, AMD , and Broadcom look likely to receive licenses to ship to China at levels consistent with the prior threshold. We would keep near-term expectations in check, given licenses have not yet been issued, and we see questions around available supply of H20 and whether RTX chips (formerly B30/B40) will also be included, but it is a significant positive for 2026 for all AI stocks, including our Top Pick NVDA . 对美国半导体行业亦是利好。中国AI资本支出规模同样接近 英伟达 预估的2025年AI加速器500亿美元市场机会。美国AI芯片公司英伟达、 AMD 和 博通 有望获得对华出口许可，规模与此前水平一致。鉴于许可证尚未发放，且H20实际供应及RTX芯片（原B30/B40）是否包含仍存疑问，我们短期保持谨慎；但对2026年所有AI股票（包括首选英伟达）构成显著利好。 --- 【段落 5】 What about other semi/tech supply chain players in Asia? TSMC chairman Dr. CC Wei, on its 2Q25 earnings call on 7/17, commented that the resumption of H20 is good news to TSMC when asked about China AI growth opportunities. We think it will be able to meet or even beat its target of a 45% CAGR for AI semi revenue in the coming five years. Samsung is a direct beneficiary of H20 resumption, and could potentially reverse a W1.5tr write-off in 2Q25 preliminary earnings due to the previous H20 shipment ban. For server ODMs, as Chinese hyperscalers are the end customers for H20 servers, we believe several server ODMs will benefit as assembly partners on the projects, including Wistron, FII and Lenovo. 亚洲其他半导体/科技供应链如何？ 台积电 董事长魏哲家在7月17日2Q25业绩会上表示，H20恢复供应对台积电是利好，当被问及中国AI增长机遇时。我们认为台积电未来五年AI半导体收入复合增速有望达到甚至超过45%的目标。三星是H20恢复供应的直接受益者，可能冲回因先前H20禁运而在2Q25初步业绩中确认的1.5万亿韩元减值。对服务器ODM而言，中国超大规模云服务商是H20服务器终端客户，我们认为纬创、富士康和联想等多家服务器ODM将受益于项目组装合作。 --- 【段落 6】 Not-so-good news for China's domestic GPU makers, but local GPU development efforts will continue. We think this news could be negative for Chinese GPU foundry SMIC if domestic GPU vendors Huawei and Cambricon lose market share with China's cloud service providers. We now expect China’s local GPU self-sufficiency to reach only 39% in 2027. However, while resumption of H20 is a constructive step forward for near-term China compute constraints, we do not see China’s efforts on self-sufficiency slowing down amid geopolitical uncertainties. 对中国本土GPU厂商而言并非利好，但本土GPU研发仍将持续。若华为、 寒武纪 等本土GPU厂商在中国云服务商中份额下降，我们认为这可能对 中芯国际 （SMIC）构成利空。我们现在预计2027年中国本土GPU自给率仅达39%。尽管如此，H20恢复供应虽缓解了短期算力瓶颈，我们并不认为中国在地缘不确定性下会放慢自主可控的努力。
